# nykaa-dashboard
2025 dashboard of nykaa
Nykaa Sales Dashboard 2025

Project Overview

This project presents an interactive sales dashboard for Nykaa,
designed to summarize sales performance, product trends, regional
revenue, brand performance, and customer order status for 2025.

The dashboard uses a pink-themed layout to make the key metrics and
visual comparisons easy to scan.

Dashboard Highlights

Sales by City Map -- Displays sales distribution across cities.

Monthly Sales Trend -- Shows how sales change across the months
of the year.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) -- Highlights total customers,
average discount, and total sales.

Revenue by Region -- Compares sales across East, South, North,
and West.

Sales by Category -- Compares categories such as Skincare,
Makeup, Fragrance, Haircare, and Personal Care.

Sales by Brand -- Ranks brands by sales.

Top 10 Best-Selling Products -- Shows the leading products by
sales.

Customer Segmentation / Order Status -- Summarizes shipped,
cancelled, returned, and delivered orders.

Purpose

The dashboard is intended to help explore sales performance and
identify:

Monthly sales patterns

Strong-performing regions and cities

Best-selling brands and products

Sales contribution by product category

Customer order-status distribution
